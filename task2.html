Class-1: Ascidiacea
Ascidiacea, commonly known as sea squirts, are marine invertebrates characterized by their sac-like bodies, tough outer tunic, and filter-feeding mechanisms.
Class-2: Thaliacea
Thaliacea is a class of marine chordates known for their unique pelagic lifestyle, complex life cycles, and filter-feeding mechanisms.
Class-3: Larvacea
Larvacea are small, transparent marine tunicates that retain a tadpole-like form throughout their lives and play a crucial role in the oceanic carbon cycle.
